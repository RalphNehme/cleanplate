How scores work

Scores run from 1 to 100. Higher is always better.

Food score

  1. Find the restaurant online. Each restaurant is matched to OpenStreetMap by name and location, which gives its real cuisine, diet options and website.
  2. Read the menu. We fetch the menu from the restaurant's own website (HTML or PDF), respecting each site's robots.txt and skipping delivery apps.
  3. AI reads every dish. Claude (an AI model by Anthropic) estimates what share of the menu is deep-fried, ultra-processed, sweets, whole-food or vegetable-forward, and copies any quality claims ("organic", "grass-fed", "house-made") and any oil the menu names, quoting the menu as evidence.
  4. A fixed formula turns that into a score. Starting at 50: up to +30 for whole foods, +10 for vegetable-forward dishes, up to +10 for quality claims, +10 for olive oil, avocado oil, butter, ghee or tallow; up to −25 for deep-fried items, −20 for ultra-processed items, −10 for sweets, −15 for seed oils named on the menu, and −5 for a fried-heavy menu that doesn't name its oil.
  5. Visitors refine it. Once {{ min_reports }} visitors report on cooking oil, ingredients and healthy options, their average counts for half of the score.

Restaurants without a readable menu aren't scored or shown, so no score is a guess from the restaurant's name or cuisine.

The seed-oil penalty is CleanPlate's own editorial choice. Major health organizations, including the American Heart Association, consider seed oils part of a healthy diet, so weigh that part of the score accordingly. AI reading can also make mistakes, so every score links to the menu it came from.

Hygiene score

Shown smaller, from each restaurant's latest health inspection.

  • New York City: violation points become 1–100 in line with the official grade (A is 85–100, B is 70–84, C is below 70). A closure by the Health Department caps it at 30.
  • Chicago: violations are weighted by severity (Priority 7, Priority Foundation 4, Core 2). A Fail caps it at 60 and a Pass with Conditions at 84. Inspections from July 2018 onward.
